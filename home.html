The Biography

Hawking Hawking: The Selling of a Scientific Celebrity

Written by Charles Seife

The book explains how Hawking really became Hawking, and all the popularity he got. The book's timeline is reversed, so it goes from his death, in 2018 to 1942. It explains why he got all of his popularity, and how he became the scientific celebrity he is. It critically explains his rise to fame and his life throughout the book.
